Common questions

How are my hours counted?

Each task you log as done counts toward its week straight away, at the time you spent on it. Your agreement with Margaret is 18 hours a week.

Who reviews my tasks?

Margaret sees every task you log and confirms it. Until she does, it shows as Awaiting review. If something doesn't look right to her, she can talk it through with you, or with us.

What if I can't do a task?

Tell Margaret as early as you can, then open the task in the Logbook and move it to another day. If it keeps happening, call us and we'll help you both find a plan that works.

We don't agree on something. What now?

Talk to Margaret first if you feel comfortable. If you don't, message or call your coordinator. We can meet with you both, at home or on a call.

If someone is hurt or in danger, call 000. Then let your coordinator know what happened.
